Delete your Rafiq Pro account

You can delete your account at any time, as a coach or as a member.

From the app

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email us from the address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my account”.

support@rafiqpro.com

How long it takes

We delete your account within 30 days of your request, whether it came from the app or by email, and confirm by email once it is done. A copy can stay in our encrypted backups for up to four weeks after that, until they are deleted.

What is deleted

Your account, your profile and your personal details — name, contact details and photos — and, for coaches, your saved payout details (national ID and wallet or bank account number).

What is kept

Sessions and payments are a shared record between a coach and a member. When one side deletes their account, the other side keeps their own record of the sessions and payments they had together, without the deleted person’s personal details.

For coaches, payout records — each amount Rafiq Pro sent you, when, and to which wallet or account — are kept as a financial record, the same way payments are.

Before you can delete

If you still have an upcoming session, unused session credits, or an open dispute, these need to be settled first — the app tells you what is outstanding, and we will too if you ask by email.
