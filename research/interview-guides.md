# Interview guides: the ten-coach test and the dive-centre check

Both strategy papers come to one cheap test: ask ten coaches to pay before
building anything else. This file is how to run it so that the answer is a
result, not an anecdote.

Ask in whichever language the coach prefers. The Arabic lines are the ones
whose wording matters most; translate the rest freely.

## Rules for every conversation

- **Ask about the past, not the future.** "Would you use…?" gets a polite
  yes from everyone. "Last week, how did you…?" gets the truth.
- **Don't pitch until the last five minutes.** The first twenty-five minutes
  are about their week, not our app.
- **End with money, not interest.** The test is a payment link, an invoice or
  a deposit. "Sounds great, send it to me" without paying counts as **no**.
- **Write the notes up within the hour**, in the scorecard below. Record
  quotes word for word.

## Who to talk to

Ten coaches who are paid today, not trainees. Aim for a spread:

| How many | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Nutrition, fitness or yoga | The volume play |
| 3 | Life, career or executive | The bridge to corporate |
| 2 | Freediving or scuba instructors | The diving idea |
| 2 | Whatever cluster appears in the first eight | Follow the signal |

Avoid friends and family where possible; if not, count their answers
separately.

## Coach interview, about 30 minutes

**1. Their practice (10 min)**
- How many paying clients do you have right now? How many a year ago?
- What does one session cost? Do clients pay per session, per package or
  monthly?
- How do clients pay you today? (Cash, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank
  transfer, card?) *Write down the exact methods; it decides Paymob's wallet
  setup.*
- Where do new clients come from? (Referral, Instagram, a clinic or studio,
  a platform?) What share from each?

**2. Last week, in detail (10 min)**
- Walk me through last week: how did you schedule sessions, set tasks,
  follow up, chase payments?
- What tools did you use? (WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Calendly, Paperbell,
  paper?) What do you pay for any of them?
- What went wrong last week? A no-show, a late payment, a client who went
  quiet?
- **The question that matters most:** what is the most annoying part of
  running your practice that is not the coaching itself?

> ما هو أكثر شيء مزعج في إدارة عملك، بعيدًا عن التدريب نفسه؟

**3. Trust and privacy (3 min)**, for relationship, stress or sleep coaches
- Do clients ever ask you to keep it private that they see you at all?
- Would a client book you through a public profile with your specialty on
  it? Or only privately, through a link?

*(The strategy review says the relationships category needs anonymous
booking. This tests that.)*

**4. The ask (7 min)**
Show the app on your phone: the roster, a client's tasks, the schedule, in
Arabic. Then:

- "This is EGP [300 or 500] a month. Here's the link. Do you want to start
  today?"

> الاشتراك [٣٠٠ / ٥٠٠] جنيه في الشهر. ده اللينك — تحب تبدأ النهارده؟

- If yes: take the payment there and then (a Paymob payment link works; no
  app purchase is needed for a test). Note the time.
- If no: "What would have to be true for you to pay that?" Write the answer
  word for word.
- Either way: "Who else should I talk to?"

**Alternate the price:** offer EGP 300 to the odd-numbered coaches and EGP
500 to the even-numbered ones, so that ten conversations test both.

**Before the first link goes out**, decide what someone who pays during the
test actually gets (for example, the coach side free until the app is in the
stores, then billed through the stores), and whether the money is a deposit
you refund if you don't launch. The advisor questions in
`payments-rules.md` still apply.

## Dive centre or instructor, about 20 minutes

This is the "how to know this analysis is wrong" check from the strategy
review: is booking actually their bottleneck?

- In high season, what limits how many divers you take: boats, instructors,
  equipment, or bookings?
- How do tourists book you today? How far ahead? What share arrive having
  booked nothing?
- How do they pay, and in what currency? Who handles the card fees?
- Do you use any booking software? What does it cost? What's wrong with it?
- If a tourist could book and pay a deposit online before flying, would that
  change anything? Name one thing that would be different next week.
- **Ask for money the same way:** "EUR [N] a month for the schedule, the
  bookings and the deposits. Want to try it this season?"

If two or three centres say boats and staff are the limit, not bookings,
the diving idea is finished. Write it down and move on.

**The gate (from the strategy review):** fewer than 3 of 10 pay means the
problem is not the product, and more code won't fix it. Three or more means
there is a business, and its price is now known. Then pick the vertical from
where the payers cluster.
